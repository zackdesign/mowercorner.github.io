A push mower fits in the boot. A ride-on doesn't — and neither does a zero-turn,
which is exactly the machine most likely to need a workshop rather than a
driveway repair. So we come and get it.

## How it works

1. **Ring the shop** on [03 5231 6466](tel:0352316466) and tell us what needs
   collecting and where you are.
2. **We book you into a run.** We're regularly out around the district, so it's
   often a matter of adding you to a trip we're already making.
3. **We collect the machine**, bring it back to the workshop at Skene Street,
   and look at it properly.
4. **We tell you what it needs** before any work starts.
5. **We bring it home** running.

## Where we go

Colac and the surrounding **Western District**. Whether a particular address is
worth a trip depends a bit on where we're already heading that week — so the
honest answer is to ring and ask rather than assume you're too far out. Farms
and acreage properties are a big part of what we do, and they're rarely next
door.

## What we'll collect

- Ride-on mowers and lawn tractors
- Zero-turn mowers
- Push and self-propelled mowers
- Other outdoor power equipment — ask if you're unsure

If you've got several machines that need doing at once — a common one on farms
and for contractors — mention it when you book. It's usually easier for
everybody to do them in one trip.

## Why it's worth it

Getting a ride-on to a workshop normally means borrowing a trailer, finding
someone to help load it, and hoping it starts at the other end. That's a
Saturday gone. It also means machines get left in the shed unserviced until
they properly break, which is always the more expensive outcome.

The [workshop](/mower-servicing-repairs/) sees the machine on the bench with
proper tools and STIHL diagnostic equipment, rather than someone crouched over
it in a paddock.
