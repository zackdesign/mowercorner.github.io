A push mower fits in the boot. A ride-on doesn't — and neither does a zero-turn,
which is exactly the machine most likely to need a workshop rather than a
driveway repair. So we come and get it.

## How it works

Ring the shop on [03 5231 6466](tel:0352316466), tell us what needs collecting
and where you are, and we'll let you know whether we can get out to you and
when. Once the machine is back at Skene Street we'll look at it properly and
tell you what it needs before any work starts.

## Where we go

Colac and the surrounding **Western District**. Rather than guess at a radius,
ring with your address — it's a quick conversation and we'd rather tell you
straight than have you assume you're too far out. Farms and acreage properties
are a big part of what we do, and they're rarely next door.

Worth knowing: the workshop gets genuinely busy, particularly heading into
spring. If your machine needs collecting, the earlier you ring the better.

## What we'll collect

- Ride-on mowers and lawn tractors
- Zero-turn mowers
- Push and self-propelled mowers
- Other outdoor power equipment — ask if you're unsure

If you've got several machines that need doing at once — a common one on farms
and for contractors — mention it when you ring. It's usually easier for
everybody to do them in one trip.

## Why it's worth it

Getting a ride-on to a workshop normally means borrowing a trailer, finding
someone to help load it, and hoping it starts at the other end. That's a
Saturday gone. It also means machines get left in the shed unserviced until
they properly break, which is always the more expensive outcome.

The [workshop](/mower-servicing-repairs/) sees the machine on the bench with
proper tools and STIHL diagnostic equipment, rather than someone crouched over
it in a paddock.
