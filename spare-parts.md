Half the reason people drive into Colac rather than order online is that the
part in your hand is worth more than a part number on a screen. Bring in the
broken one, or the machine, and we'll match it at the counter.

## What we keep on the shelf

We hold a **massive range of spare parts** across the brands we sell and
service. The things that wear out and the things that break:

- **Mower blades** — swing-back, bar and mulching blades, plus bolts and bosses
- **Belts** — drive and cutter belts for push mowers and ride-ons
- **Filters** — air, fuel and oil
- **Spark plugs** for two-stroke and four-stroke engines
- **Chainsaw chains and bars** in a range of pitches and lengths
- **Trimmer line and heads**
- **Cables, controls and throttle assemblies**
- **Carburettor kits and diaphragms**
- **Two-stroke oil, bar and chain oil, fuel additives**

As an **Elite STIHL Dealer** we also stock STIHL machinery, accessories and a
large range of genuine STIHL parts.

## Bring in the part, or the model number

The quickest way to get the right part is to bring in the old one. Failing that,
the model number off the compliance plate or the engine will usually get us
there — a photo on your phone is fine. Guessing from memory is where it tends to
go wrong, and a mower blade that's a few millimetres out is a mower blade you
can't use.

If it's not on the shelf, we'll tell you honestly whether we can get it in and
roughly how long it'll take. Some parts for older machines are genuinely no
longer made, and it's better to know that up front than to wait weeks for
nothing.

## Fitting it for you

Some jobs are a five-minute change in the driveway. Others — carburettor
rebuilds, drive belts on a ride-on, anything involving the deck coming
off — are quicker and safer in a workshop with the right tools. If you'd rather
we did it, [our workshop](/mower-servicing-repairs/) is on the same site, and
we'll quote it before we start.

## Brands we carry parts for
